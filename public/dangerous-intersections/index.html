Key Findings

  • Fatalities doubled year-over-year: 10 (2022) → 15 (2023) → 20 (2024)
  • Oceanside ranks #1 out of 62 comparable cities for alcohol-involved collisions (OTS 2023)
  • Also #1/62 for young drunk drivers (ages 21-34) and motorcycle collisions
  • Coast Highway is the most dangerous corridor for pedestrians and motorcyclists
  • Oceanside Blvd is the most dangerous east-west corridor across all crash types
  • 12% of all intersection crashes in San Diego County occur in Oceanside

Methodology Note

Rankings 1-12 are based on reported crash counts from SWITRS data (2021-2024) and the Miller Steele San Diego County intersection study. Rankings 13-20 are identified hotspots from TIMS, OTS, and law enforcement data without precise countywide crash counts available. Entries marked with badges indicate documented pedestrian, bicycle, or motorcycle crash clusters at that location.
