## Article

**Title:** Why North County Needs More Housing

**Description:** How zoning reform and missing middle housing can make San Diego's North Coast more affordable and livable for everyone.

**Author:** NC YIMBY

**Body:**

Across North County, rising rents and home prices are pushing out longtime residents, essential workers, and young families. The root cause is simple: we aren't building enough homes.

Cities like Oceanside, Carlsbad, Encinitas, and Vista have some of the most restrictive zoning in the state. Most residential land is zoned exclusively for single-family homes, making it illegal to build the duplexes, triplexes, and small apartment buildings that once defined our neighborhoods.

Missing middle housing fills the gap between single-family homes and large apartment complexes. These are the modest, neighborhood-scale buildings that allow more people to live near jobs, transit, and community resources without changing the character of a street.

California YIMBY's AB 1070, authored by Assemblymember Chris Ward, directs state agencies to study how using residential building codes for small multi-family projects could accelerate the construction of missing middle housing. It's a practical step toward making it easier and cheaper to build the homes our communities need.

Recent state legislation like SB 9 and AB 1070 is opening the door, but local action matters. Attend a city council meeting, join NC YIMBY, and help us build a North County where everyone can afford to live.
