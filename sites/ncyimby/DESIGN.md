# Overview

Community organizing site for North Coastal YIMBY Dems SD, a pro-housing advocacy group in San Diego's North Coast. The design should feel warm, approachable, and civic without being institutional. The audience is neighbors and potential housing advocates.

# Colors

Warm palette built on burnt orange and cream. Deep brown (`--text-primary`) on pale cream (`--bg-primary`) for high readability. Burnt orange accent (`--accent-primary`) for buttons and links. Warm tan for rules and dividers. No cool tones; everything stays in the amber/orange family.

# Typography

Headlines should carry civic weight without stiffness. Body text clean and readable. Generous line height for long-form articles. All font sizes from the `--fontSize-*` scale.

# Layout

Content-first layout. Comfortable reading width (~720px) for articles. Generous vertical spacing between sections. Cards and content blocks use minimal decoration; the warm palette provides enough visual separation.

# Components

Buttons: `--accent-primary` background with `--btn-text` label. Minimal border radius. Links: underline on hover, same accent color. Cards: light or no border, warm background. Newsletter signup: prominent but not aggressive.

# Do's and Don'ts

- DO: Keep the warm, inviting tone in every surface. Every element should feel like it belongs to the same sun-warmed family.
- DO: Use whitespace generously. Civic content needs room to breathe.
- DON'T: Use cool blues, grays, or any color outside the warm spectrum.
- DON'T: Use heavy shadows, gradients, or dark mode. The cream-and-orange identity is the brand.
